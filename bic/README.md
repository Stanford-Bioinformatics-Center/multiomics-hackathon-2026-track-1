## Dataset

- Study: **Acute Exercise in Human Sedentary Adults**, `human-precovid-sed-adu`.
- Data collection: **c2.0**, human pre-suspension cohort, 175 adults across the study.
- Public package: `MotrpacHumanPreSuspensionAnalysis` **2.0.8**, dated 2026-09-23.
- Pinned commit: `535b4044e7417413de471104c619120337602b77`.
- Sources: [human collection](https://motrpac-data.org/data-download/file-browser/analysis/human-precovid-sed-adu/c2.0), [pinned package](https://github.com/MoTrPAC/MotrpacHumanPreSuspensionAnalysis/tree/535b4044e7417413de471104c619120337602b77).

The collection measures responses to a single acute exercise bout. It does not establish training adaptation, secretion from a particular tissue, or protection from disease.
